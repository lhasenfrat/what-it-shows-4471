The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for. Fix: read dates in the tenant’s own order, which dayFirst() in causes.ts already asks of the platform.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first. Under its date field, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY; booking-v2 prints DD/MM/YYYY. On a day-first tenant, people type what the hint says and the form reads it the tenant’s way, so the date flips just like a parser report. It only happens through the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it. Fix: print the tenant’s order under the field, as booking-v2 does.

one-day: the date moves by one day. These reports describe a booking that lands a day off, not a day and month swapped: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json), but CauseTable files them under parser because of their tenant. A parser that reads month first cannot move a date by one day, so neither fix above touches them. It looks like a time-zone conversion; that needs checking.

Why one ticket hid three

  • The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). All three problems come in under the same subjects (“Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”), so they all merged into #4471.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The “tagged as” column shows how often it disagrees with the problem.
  • The ticket export comes in pages that overlap, so some reports appear twice. Count by id, once.

For the desk

  • Keep #4471 open until all three tickets are closed, and re-read this table when a fix ships: it is built from the export, so it shows what is left.
  • A report about a wrong date is not a duplicate just because its subject matches. Check the tenant, the channel and what moved (a day and month swapped, or one day) before you merge.
  • Decision 0001 still says to merge on subject alone. A new decision record should replace it.
